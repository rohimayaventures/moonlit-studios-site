**"Will AI make my business feel impersonal?"**
Only if you let it. Good AI enhances human connection by handling routine tasks so you can focus on relationship-building.

**"I'm not tech-savvy—is this too complicated?"**
Modern AI tools are designed for non-technical users. And if you work with Moonlit Studios, I handle all technical aspects while training you in plain English.

**"What if AI makes mistakes?"**
AI should be supervised, especially at first. Use it as an assistant, not a replacement. Always review important outputs.

**"Isn't this expensive?"**
Free and low-cost tools exist. Custom development is an investment—but if you value your time at $50-$100/hour, the ROI is undeniable.
