## 🎯 LEAD SCORING SYSTEM

### Automatic Lead Scoring (Notion Formula)

```
SCORE =
  (Budget Points) +
  (Timeline Points) +
  (Industry Points) +
  (Engagement Points) +
  (Source Points)

Budget Points:
- Under $5k = 2
- $5k-10k = 5
- $10k-20k = 8
- $20k+ = 10

Timeline Points:
- ASAP (1-2 months) = 10
- 2-4 months = 7
- 4-6 months = 4
- Just exploring = 1

Industry Points:
- Healthcare = 10 (your specialty)
- Tech/SaaS = 8
- Small business = 6
- Other = 4

Engagement Points:
- Tried 3+ AI demos = 8
- Requested quote = 7
- Booked consultation = 10
- Read 3+ blog posts = 5
- Opened 3+ emails = 4
- Quiz completion = 6

Source Points:
- Referral = 10
- LinkedIn outreach response = 8
- Organic (AI Lab, blog) = 7
- Paid ad = 4
```

### Lead Temperature Tags

🔥 **HOT (30+ points):**
- Healthcare + high budget + ASAP timeline
- **Action:** Personal outreach within 1 hour
- **Priority:** Drop everything
- **Goal:** Book call same day

⚡ **WARM (20-29 points):**
- Good fit but slower timeline or mid budget
- **Action:** Follow up within 24 hours
- **Priority:** Schedule call within 3 days
- **Goal:** Move to proposal stage

☀️ **LUKEWARM (10-19 points):**
- Some interest but unclear fit
- **Action:** Nurture via email sequence
- **Priority:** Check in weekly
- **Goal:** Warm them up over 2-4 weeks

❄️ **COLD (0-9 points):**
- Low budget, just exploring, poor fit
- **Action:** Add to long-term nurture
- **Priority:** Monthly check-in
- **Goal:** Stay top of mind for 6+ months
