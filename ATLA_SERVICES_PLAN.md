# ATLA Services Page Transformation Plan

## Element Mapping

### 1. Water Bending - Creative Design & Development
- **Philosophy**: "Be like water" - Adaptable, flowing, takes the shape of any container
- **Colors**: Blue gradients (#4A9B9B mermaidTeal, #3AA7A3 tealBright)
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate Bender
- **Description**: Like water flowing around obstacles, creativity that adapts while maintaining its essence

### 2. Earth Bending - Health x Tech Development
- **Philosophy**: Solid foundation, neutral jing (listening and waiting), grounded strength
- **Colors**: Green/Earth tones (#2D5016 earth green, browns)
- **Difficulty Level**: Master Bender
- **Description**: Rooted in clinical reality, building stable healthcare platforms with the strength of stone

### 3. Air Bending - Consulting
- **Philosophy**: Freedom, detachment, wisdom from above
- **Colors**: Light grays, whites (#C5D5E4 starlight)
- **Difficulty Level**: Novice Path (most accessible)
- **Description**: Strategic guidance that flows freely, seeing perspectives others miss

### 4. Fire Bending - AI Innovation Suite
- **Philosophy**: Power, drive, transformation through intensity
- **Colors**: Orange/Red (#FF6B35 phoenixFire, #FFD700 lunarGold)
- **Difficulty Level**: Master Bender
- **Description**: Transformative AI that burns away the old, forging new possibilities

### 5. Spirit Bending - Author & Ghostwriting Studio
- **Philosophy**: Connection to voice, balance between worlds, spiritual harmony
- **Colors**: Purple/Gold ethereal tones
- **Difficulty Level**: Intermediate Bender
- **Description**: Channeling your authentic voice, bridging the spirit of your story with readers
